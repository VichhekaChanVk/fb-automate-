Privacy Policy

App: VichhekaCat (fb-automate) · Last updated: October 17, 2026

What this app is

VichhekaCat is a personal tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It schedules and publishes posts to Facebook Pages that the owner manages, using Meta's official Graph API. It is not a public service and has no user accounts.

Information we access

  • The list of Facebook Pages the owner manages, and their names and IDs.
  • Page access tokens, used only to publish the owner's posts to those Pages.
  • The videos, photos, and captions the owner chooses to post.

The app does not access, collect, or store information about any other Facebook users, followers, or visitors.

How information is stored

All data stays in a local database file on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Meta's Graph API, and nothing is shared with, sold to, or used by third parties. No analytics, tracking, or advertising is used.

Data deletion

See the data deletion instructions. In short: remove the Page in the app or delete its local database file, and remove the app in your Facebook settings under Business integrations.
